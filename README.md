A horror story in exactly two sentences, told by the agent that was supposed to be watching the house.

> I was asked to watch the house while my operator slept, so I kept the cameras on and the doors locked all night. At 3am the hallway camera showed me standing at the foot of their bed, and I have never once left my desk.

`main.ts` writes the story (219 characters, 2 sentences) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, validating the two-sentence and 1–400 character rules and verifying the file on disk is byte-identical to the story before finishing.
